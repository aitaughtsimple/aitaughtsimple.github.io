AI cheat sheet

From your AI Taught Simple class

How to ask

  1. Say what you need. Who is it for, and what should it do?
  2. Add details. How long, how formal, any names or dates.
  3. Ask for changes. "Make it shorter." "Simpler, please." "Try again."

Things to try

  • "Write a short, polite email asking to move my appointment."
  • "How do I make the text bigger on my phone? Give me steps."
  • "Plan a relaxed day trip for two people who don't want to walk much."
  • "I have chicken, rice, and spinach. What can I make for two?"
  • "I got this text: [describe it]. Does it look like a scam?" AI can spot warning signs, but it can't prove a message is real.

Stay safe

Never type into an AI chat

  • Passwords or PINs
  • Bank or card numbers
  • Your Social Security number

Scam warning signs

  • Rushing you to act right now
  • Asking for gift cards, crypto, Bitcoin ATMs, wire transfers, or cash
  • Telling you to move money to a "safe" account or keep it secret
  • Links that don't match the real website
  • A loved one's voice asking for money

What to do

  • Hang up. Call back on a number from your card, bill, or contacts, never one an AI or caller gave you
  • Agree on a family safe word
  • Forward scam texts to 7726, then delete them
  • Double-check health and money answers
